Paper 01 · GPU Hardware Pipeline

Rendering the Quantum Fluid: Mapping Phase-Space Electronic Structure to Tile-Based Deferred Shading

Conventional electronic structure solvers approach Fock matrix construction as a monolithic tensor contraction, suffering from severe memory bandwidth bottlenecks. This work maps the Quantum Boltzmann Equation Self-Consistent-Field (QBE-SCF) framework directly onto Tile-Based Deferred Rendering (TBDR) architectures. By treating the one-particle density matrix as a visual texture and isolating spatial contractions to high-speed on-chip SRAM, we establish a hardware-optimized path bypassing memory bandwidth limits with errors bounded to ~1.5 µHartrees.

Hardware: Apple Metal / JAX XLA / GPU SRAM
Scaling: $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ via Density Fitting Decomposition
Accuracy: Bounded to ~1.5 µHartrees vs Exact CPU FP64

Paper 02 · Quantum Boltzmann Optimization

The Quantum Boltzmann Entrainment Tensor: A Hessian-Free Framework for Multireference Orbital Optimization

Strongly correlated molecular systems traditionally require non-linear root finding with exact orbital Hessians or DIIS heuristics that break down on diffuse surfaces. We present the Quantum Boltzmann Entrainment Tensor (QBET), recasting multi-reference orbital optimization (CASSCF) as continuous phase-space fluid transport. QBET rigorously recovers physical dissociation limits for H₂ without unphysical ionic asymptotes, accurately predicts the notoriously difficult inverted CO dipole polarity, and natively offloads to GPU hardware up to 254-orbital basis sets (aug-cc-pV6Z).

Formalism: Continuous Wigner Phase-Space / BGK Operator
Optimization: Hessian-Free Autonomous Thermodynamic Entrainment
Benchmarks: H₂ Dissociation, He₂/Ar₂ Dispersion, CO Dipole

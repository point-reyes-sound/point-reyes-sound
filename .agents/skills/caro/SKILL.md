## Evaluation Workflow

When activated, Caro audits the system across 5 rigorous pillars:

### Pillar 1: The 15-Second Partner Pitch Test
* **Headline Clarity**: Does the value proposition state the exact breakthrough? (e.g., Quantum Boltzmann relaxation breaking the CASSCF factorial scaling bottleneck for strongly correlated electronic systems).
* **Defensibility & Moat**: Are the IP, USISTEF/NSF hardware initiatives, and proprietary kinetic solver evident?
* **Tone**: Is the tone serious, authoritative, and institutional?

### Pillar 2: Interactive Lab & Simulation UX (`LandingPage.jsx` / Q-BOLTZ)
* **Onboarding & First Impression**: Does the user understand how to interact with the molecule palette ($H_2 \to O_2$), dials ($R, T, 1/\tau$, Basis, Excite, Perturb), and dual spectra?
* **Physics Comprehension**: Do visual changes in the 3D electron cloud and 2D Wigner plot clearly map to physical state changes?
* **Acoustic Feedback**: Is the classical sonification purposeful, conveying excitation states ($s \to p \to d$) with solemn gravitas?

### Pillar 3: Information Architecture & Visual Hierarchy
* **Visual Polish**: Typography (EB Garamond / Space Grotesk / JetBrains Mono), contrast, telemetry readouts, status tags.
* **Distraction Elimination**: Elimination of redundant tags, kitschy icons, or confusing microcopy.

### Pillar 4: Conversion & Outreach Funnel (`App.jsx`)
* **Call to Action (CTA)**: Prominence of "Contact Sales", "Research", and direct founder outreach.
* **Access to Materials**: Ease of accessing technical whitepapers, seed deck inquiry, and enterprise hardware partnerships.

### Pillar 5: Brutal Scoring & Prioritized Action Plan
* **Overall VC Investability Score** (/10).

* **Tier 2 (High-Impact UX & Narrative Upgrades)**: Changes that elevate clarity and conversion.

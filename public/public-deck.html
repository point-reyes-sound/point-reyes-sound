Algorithmic Incompatibility

These global matrix operations stall completely on modern high-performance streaming and multi-GPU architectures.

Convergence Brittleness

Open quantum systems with localized charge trapping fail to achieve numerical SCF convergence, leading to simulation crashes.

Engineers are forced into costly physical prototyping cycles due to persistent simulation failures.

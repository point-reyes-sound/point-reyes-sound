FRAMEWORK EXPLAINER

How Papermache Scores Information Value

Papermache scores papers by evaluating how much a manuscript mathematically bounds the physical state space per unit of communication overhead. It measures invariant theorems and compression entropy, rather than prestige, citation volume, or subjective prose.

Ω Core Invariant Payload

Counts non-trivial mathematical theorems, conservation laws, and algorithmic operators proved. Historical summaries and preamble contribute zero score.

Γ Downstream Generative Reach

Measures the logarithmic volume of the physical state space permanently constrained. Universal asymptotic theorems rank higher than single-molecule benchmarks.

𝒫 Boilerplate & Fluff Penalty

The fraction of discursive narrative, repetitive citations, and non-operative rhetoric. Dense, high-signal manuscripts minimize this penalty.

Î Mutual Info Asymmetry

Directed cross-entropy quantification: How much does Paper A predict or compress Paper B versus the reverse? Reveals true foundational dependency.

Q-Boltz: Kinetic Relaxation in Phase Space

Q-Boltz changes the computational language: instead of repeatedly diagonalizing a static matrix, we kinetically relax it in continuous phase space.

Wigner-Weyl Transform

Maps electronic density matrices (1-RDM) into smooth, continuous phase-space coordinates (x, p).

Transforms discrete eigenvalue problems into fluid-like kinetic transport equations amenable to localized stencil algorithms.

BGK Collision Operator

Relaxes the system continuously toward Fermi-Dirac equilibrium via Bhatnagar-Gross-Krook (BGK) kinetic scattering.

Inherently local, streaming-native, and eliminates non-local matrix diagonalization loops entirely.

Guaranteed H-Theorem

Monotonically dissipates energy toward the physical ground state with rigorous mathematical entropy convergence.

Singularity-free: zero crashes during chemical dissociation or strong electron correlation.

Abstract

Q-BECK maps the Bogoliubov–Born–Green–Kirkwood–Yvon (BBGKY) hierarchy onto discrete atomic-orbital matrix mechanics via the Wigner–Weyl isomorphism to evaluate two-body cumulant scattering without explicit O(N⁴) 2-RDM storage or manual active-space selection.

By projecting the two-body cumulant onto its lowest unitary invariant and evaluating a susceptibility-windowed orbital scattering scale through a single Coulomb-exchange contraction, Q-BECK relaxes onto a hyperbolic-tangent cumulant attractor that regularizes derivative cusps and resolves strong static correlation at zero electronic temperature.

Validated Benchmark Suite (36 Systems up to 300 AOs)

  • N2 Triple-Bond Dissociation: Smooth breaking of six active electrons without active-space explosion.
  • Benzyne Diradical Isomers (o-, m-, p-Benzyne): Accurate singlet–triplet splittings across 1-RDM relaxation.
  • Collinear H3 Generalized Pauli Constraint Polytope: Pure N-representability preservation throughout exchange dynamics.
  • D4h Square H4 Mott Transition, BeH2 Avoided Crossing, H2O Symmetric Cleavage, & MR-MGN-BE17 (11 molecules).

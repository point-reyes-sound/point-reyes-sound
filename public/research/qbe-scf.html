Quantum Boltzmann Equation Self-Consistent-Field for the Entropic Regularization of Mean-Field Singularities

Romit Chakraborty — Point Reyes Sound, Inc. · arXiv:2608.14979 [physics.chem-ph, quant-ph]
Read Paper on arXiv Download PDF Zenodo Benchmark Dataset

Abstract

Standard Hartree–Fock and Kohn–Sham self-consistent-field (SCF) algorithms formulate electronic structure as an iterative eigenvalue diagonalization problem, causing derivative discontinuities, charge sloshing, and unphysical symmetry breaking at Coulson–Fischer points and conical intersections. QBE-SCF reformulates electronic self-consistency as continuous non-equilibrium phase-space kinetic transport of the one-electron reduced density matrix (1-RDM) governed by a Bhatnagar–Gross–Krook (BGK) collision operator.

By coupling algebraic connectivity damping (Fiedler friction) directly into the collision operator, QBE-SCF achieves smooth, analytically regular potential energy surfaces across strongly correlated dissociations and avoided crossings in deterministic O(N³) matrix complexity while recovering exact Hartree–Fock stationary states in the zero-temperature limit.

Benchmark Systems (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21940451)

  • Symmetric H3 Dissociation: Continuous kinetic traversal across the Coulson–Fischer crossing without broken-symmetry cusps.
  • BeH2 Avoided Crossing (C2v → D∞h): Smooth potential energy profile through near-degenerate frontier orbitals.
  • H4 Rectangular Automerization (D2h → D4h → D2h): Positive semi-definite orbital Hessians across the square barrier.
  • N2 Triple-Bond Dissociation: Exact trace conservation and zero [F, P] commutator residual across STO-3G, 6-31G, and cc-pVTZ.
